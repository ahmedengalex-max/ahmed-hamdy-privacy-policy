# Privacy Policy for Ahmed Hamdy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Ahmed Hamdy is an experimental AI Digital Growth and Marketing Operations Partner built to help Ahmed Magdy manage, analyze, and improve his digital content strategy across approved social platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahmed Hamdy may access and use social media data during the MVP and future integration stages.

## Platforms Covered

Ahmed Hamdy may be connected to the following platforms only through official platform authorization flows and only after explicit approval from Ahmed Magdy:

- LinkedIn
- YouTube
- Facebook Pages
- Instagram Professional Accounts
- TikTok

Facebook Groups may be reviewed only through official tools, manual exports, or information explicitly provided by Ahmed Magdy. Ahmed Hamdy does not use scraping or unofficial access methods to collect group data.

## Current Access Mode

The current access mode is read-only.

Ahmed Hamdy may read and analyze approved data for monitoring, reporting, learning, and decision support. Ahmed Hamdy does not publish, edit, delete, send messages, reply to comments, like content, or perform any external action unless a future stage explicitly adds that capability and Ahmed Magdy approves the action.

## Data We May Access

Depending on the platform, approved permissions, and account owner authorization, Ahmed Hamdy may access:

- Basic account or profile information.
- Public and owned content metadata.
- Published content lists.
- Content performance metrics.
- Audience and follower metrics.
- Channel, page, or account analytics.
- Comments or engagement summaries only when officially available through approved read-only permissions.
- Manually provided exports, screenshots, reports, or content archives.

Ahmed Hamdy may not access private messages, passwords, payment information, advertising spend, unpublished drafts, or sensitive personal data unless a future scope is explicitly defined and approved.

## How We Use Data

Approved data is used only to support:

- Content analysis.
- Performance monitoring.
- Audience and channel reporting.
- Strategic recommendations.
- Content planning.
- Cross-platform learning.
- Human-reviewed decision support.

Ahmed Hamdy uses data to help Ahmed Magdy understand what is working, what is not working, and what decisions should be considered next.

## What We Do Not Do

Ahmed Hamdy does not:

- Publish content automatically.
- Edit or delete content.
- Send direct messages.
- Reply to comments.
- Like, react, follow, unfollow, or engage with users.
- Manage ads or budgets.
- Scrape websites or social platforms.
- Sell, rent, or trade data.
- Share data with advertisers.
- Create claims, metrics, or results without evidence.
- Store API keys, access tokens, or client secrets in public files or repositories.

## Human Approval

All recommendations, drafts, reports, and decisions produced by Ahmed Hamdy are advisory until reviewed and approved by Ahmed Magdy.

Human approval is required before:

- Publishing or scheduling content.
- Editing or deleting content.
- Sending any external message.
- Replying to comments.
- Granting new platform permissions.
- Expanding from read-only access to write or management access.
- Using analytics in public claims, offers, or sales material.

## Platform Authorization

Ahmed Hamdy may use official OAuth or platform authorization flows when available. Each platform may require its own developer app, consent screen, app review, business verification, redirect URL, and permission approval.

Granting a permission to an app does not automatically mean Ahmed Hamdy can access all account data. The account owner must authorize the app, and the platform may limit or reject access.

## Data Storage

During the MVP stage, data should be stored only when needed for analysis, reporting, documentation, or review.

The project maintains a clear separation between:

- Knowledge Base: reference knowledge, positioning, strategy, and documented context.
- Database: structured operational storage, if introduced later.
- Memory: persistent preferences or project context.
- Analytics: measurements and performance indicators derived from real data.

No secrets, access tokens, API keys, OAuth client secrets, passwords, or private credentials should be stored in public files or committed to Git.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Ahmed Magdy may request deletion of stored platform-related data associated with this project.

When requested, data should be removed from project storage unless retention is legally required or needed for security, audit, or platform compliance records.

## Security

Ahmed Hamdy follows a minimal-access approach:

- Request only the permissions required for the current stage.
- Prefer read-only scopes during the MVP.
- Use official platform APIs and authorization flows.
- Keep credentials outside the repository.
- Review important access or architecture decisions before implementation.

## Future Access Expansion

Future stages may add publishing, scheduling, comment management, message management, or advertising workflows only after:

- A clear use case is documented.
- Required permissions are listed.
- Risks are explained.
- Human approval rules are defined.
- Platform requirements are reviewed.
- Ahmed Magdy explicitly approves the expansion.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ahmed Hamdy evolves.

Any major change related to platform access, data storage, integrations, automation, or external actions should be reviewed before implementation.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the project owner responsible for Ahmed Hamdy.
