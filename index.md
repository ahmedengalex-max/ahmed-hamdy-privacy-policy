# Privacy Policy for Ahmed Hamdy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Ahmed Hamdy is an experimental AI Digital Growth & Marketing Operations Partner built to help Ahmed Magdy manage, analyze, and improve his LinkedIn content strategy.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ahmed Hamdy may access and use LinkedIn-related data during the initial MVP stage.

## What Data We Access

Ahmed Hamdy is designed to use LinkedIn data in read-only mode only.

Depending on the permissions granted by LinkedIn and the account owner, the system may access:

- Basic LinkedIn profile information, such as name, profile identifier, and profile image.
- Email address, if explicitly provided by LinkedIn through the approved sign-in flow.
- LinkedIn content performance analytics, if LinkedIn grants the required permissions.
- LinkedIn follower or profile analytics, if LinkedIn grants the required permissions.
- LinkedIn posts or content data only if made available through official LinkedIn APIs or manually provided by the account owner.

## How We Use This Data

LinkedIn data is used only to support:

- Content analysis.
- LinkedIn performance review.
- Content strategy recommendations.
- Growth and positioning insights.
- Human-reviewed planning and decision support.

Ahmed Hamdy does not use LinkedIn data for automated posting, messaging, commenting, liking, or any external action.

## What We Do Not Do

Ahmed Hamdy does not:

- Publish LinkedIn posts.
- Send LinkedIn messages.
- Add comments or likes.
- Automate external LinkedIn activity.
- Scrape LinkedIn pages.
- Sell LinkedIn data.
- Share LinkedIn data with advertisers.
- Create claims or performance results without real data.
- Use permissions that were not explicitly approved.

## Human Approval

Any recommendation, draft, or insight generated by Ahmed Hamdy is considered advisory only.

No external action is taken without explicit human approval from the account owner.

## Data Storage

During the MVP stage, data may be stored only when needed for analysis, documentation, or review.

The project keeps a clear separation between:

- Knowledge Base
- Database
- Memory
- Analytics

No secrets, access tokens, API keys, or private credentials should be stored in public files or repositories.

## Third-Party Services

Ahmed Hamdy may use official LinkedIn APIs only after proper authorization through LinkedIn OAuth or other approved LinkedIn access methods.

No unofficial scraping or unauthorized access methods are used.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data.

Data may only be reviewed by the project owner or authorized human reviewers for the purpose of improving LinkedIn content strategy and analysis.

## Data Deletion

The account owner may request deletion of stored LinkedIn-related data associated with this project.

When requested, data should be removed from project storage unless retention is legally required or technically necessary for security logs.

## Security

Ahmed Hamdy follows a minimal-access approach.

Only the permissions required for read-only analysis should be requested. Write permissions, publishing permissions, messaging permissions, and engagement permissions are outside the MVP scope.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the project evolves.

Any major change related to data access, storage, integrations, or automation should be reviewed before implementation.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the project owner responsible for Ahmed Hamdy.
